## Generierungs-Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production iPhone and iPad app icon, a single full-bleed square 1024 x 1024 image. Create an original, beautiful, highly legible icon for Roadbook, a personal motorcycle travel companion with routes and a private travel diary. Visual family observed in the user's other app icons: vivid warm vermilion orange background approximately #F04B0B with extremely subtle soft tonal texture, and a single warm ivory #FFF9F0 symbol; modern minimal bold flat silhouette, not 3D. Main symbol: a generously sized open travel journal/book seen straight-on, with two gently curved solid ivory pages, and a graceful winding orange road integrated as negative space through its central area. The open book silhouette must read instantly; the gently winding road is one simple continuous cutout, no dashed markings. Emotion: open roads, freedom, memories. Balanced centred composition with about 15 percent safe margin on every side; a strong memorable graphic readable at 40 pixels. Keep very simple with large bold shapes and clean edges. No lettering, no initials, no wordmark, no motorcycle illustration, no pins, no compass, no arrow tip, no extra pictograms, no border, no shadow, no extruded objects, no mockup, no surrounding canvas, no rounded outer corners: orange fills the entire square edge to edge, fully opaque. Deliver just the finished icon.
